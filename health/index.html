Everything You Need to Know About Our Health Software Solutions

Got questions about white-label healthcare software, telemedicine development, or working with TodayInTech? We've got answers.

What kind of custom software do you build? +

We build all types of custom software, including HealthTech platforms, EdTech systems, FinTech dashboards, and eCommerce solutions. We specialize in rapidly deploying scalable MVPs in 1-2 Weeks, saving you significant time and budget compared to traditional development cycles while maintaining full ownership and customization control.

How much does it cost to build a custom software application? +

The cost depends on complexity and features. We focus on lean, high-value MVPs starting from just $99 to get you to market quickly and affordably. A comprehensive enterprise platform with complex API integrations and custom dashboards can scale appropriately with your budget. Book a free consultation for a custom quote tailored to your specific requirements. See our white-label vs. custom healthcare software comparison for a full cost and timeline breakdown.

Are your healthcare solutions HIPAA and GDPR compliant? +

Absolutely. All healthcare products built by TodayInTech are fully HIPAA compliant and can be configured for GDPR compliance. Our security measures include end-to-end encryption (AES-256), secure cloud storage on AWS with BAA agreements, role-based access controls, comprehensive audit logging, regular penetration testing, and SOC 2 compliance readiness. We follow healthcare industry best practices to ensure complete patient data protection. For the full regulatory picture — including state telehealth licensure and payer reimbursement rules — see our white-label telehealth platform guide.

How long does it take to develop a telemedicine or healthcare app? +

With our white-label approach, a fully branded telemedicine app can be launched in 1-2 Weeks. Custom telemedicine platforms with advanced features like AI-powered diagnostics, multi-provider scheduling, EHR integration, and insurance claim processing typically take 3-6 months. Our agile development process with 2-week sprints ensures transparency, rapid iteration, and delivery without compromising quality or security. Walk through the exact process in our 5-step white-label telehealth implementation guide.

What technologies does TodayInTech use for app development? +

We use industry-leading technologies chosen for scalability and performance: React & Next.js for web apps, React Native & Flutter for cross-platform mobile apps, Node.js & Python for backend APIs, MongoDB & PostgreSQL for databases, AWS & GCP for cloud infrastructure, Docker & Kubernetes for containerization, and HL7 FHIR for healthcare data interoperability. Every tech decision is made to ensure long-term maintainability and scalability.

Do you provide post-launch support and maintenance? +

Yes, we provide comprehensive 24/7 post-launch support including bug fixes, performance monitoring, security patches, feature enhancements, server management, and dedicated account management. We offer flexible support plans — from basic maintenance to full managed services. We believe in building long-term partnerships with our clients, which is why 95% of our clients continue working with us after their initial project launch.

Can you integrate with existing hospital systems like Epic, Cerner, or Allscripts? +

Yes, we specialize in healthcare system integrations. Our team has extensive experience integrating with major EHR systems including Epic, Cerner, Allscripts, and athenahealth via HL7 FHIR APIs. We also handle integrations with pharmacy networks (Surescripts), insurance providers, payment gateways (Stripe, Square), lab systems, medical device APIs, and wearable platforms (Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit). See what's next for these integrations in our rundown of 2026 technology trends shaping white-label telehealth, including remote patient monitoring and AI-assisted clinical workflows.

What industries does TodayInTech serve beyond healthcare? +

While healthcare is our core specialization, we also build exceptional software for fintech, e-commerce, education (EdTech), fitness & wellness, logistics, and enterprise SaaS. Our expertise in building secure, scalable, and compliant systems translates perfectly across regulated industries. With 120+ projects delivered across 12+ countries, we bring cross-industry insights to every project.
