The Problem

When organizing prestige industry galas, managing awards portal development with open public nominations, confidential judging panels, and premier sponsorship showcases created severe administrative complexity. The Guernsey Property & Construction Awards needed a prestigious digital home to celebrate architectural and engineering achievements across the Channel Islands.

  • Disorganized nomination submissions with large architectural photo and document attachments
  • Risk of duplicate votes and ballot tampering during public voting phases
  • Lack of dedicated digital prominence for corporate headline sponsors
  • Manual ticketing and guest registration processes for the annual black-tie gala dinner

Our Solution

We developed an all-in-one event voting web platform and industry showcase featuring structured multi-category nomination portals, secure ballot fraud prevention, rich multimedia nominee galleries, and premier sponsor spotlight sections.

  • Seamless multi-step nomination portal with drag-and-drop architectural portfolio uploads
  • Secure IP and email-verified judging and voting authentication engine
  • Comprehensive Hall of Fame showcasing past award recipients and project milestones
  • Dynamic sponsor integration with tiered logo placement and corporate profiles
  • Mobile-first responsive design ensuring flawless display during live gala proceedings

The Result

The platform elevated the prestige of the Guernsey Property & Construction Awards to new heights, establishing record engagement across Channel Island property developers.

  • +300% increase in online category nomination submissions
  • 10,000+ verified votes cast during the public engagement window
  • Sold-out gala dinner with automated seat reservations and attendee management
  • Zero downtime during peak voting and announcement traffic surges
