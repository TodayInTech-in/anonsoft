TodayInTech SITES Church Management Software FAQs

What is Church Management Software (ChMS)?

Church Management Software (ChMS) is a specialized database system designed to help ministries automate day-to-day operations. It consolidates member tracking, small groups, online tithing, volunteer service planning, child classroom check-in, and fund-based church accounting under one unified platform.

How does online giving integrate with ChMS?

Our giving module syncs in real-time with your member profiles directory. When a member submits a donation via the web form or mobile app, the ledger log updates their tithing records instantly, and allocates the amount to designated funds for automated year-end tax statements.

Is child check-in secure and compliant?

Absolutely. The check-in station prints matching randomized barcode badges for both children and guardians. Labels are matched upon pickup. The system also supports automatic background checks via integrations to protect kids' classrooms.

Can we migrate our data from older databases?

Yes. Our engineers coordinate full spreadsheet and database migrations from older software packages, ensuring family records, group memberships, and historical tithing ledgers transfer completely with zero data loss or downtime.

Do you support multi-campus churches?

Yes. Our platform scales from single-site ministries to multi-site campuses. Large configurations feature location switchers that filter directories, events, giving accounts, and staff roles by campus location, while maintaining central organizational reports.
