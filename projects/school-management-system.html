TodayInTech SITES School Management Software FAQs

What is a School Management System (SMS)?

A School Management System is a comprehensive ERP platform that automates administrative and academic tasks. It unites student registration, fee invoicing, exam gradebooks, timetables, bus route tracking, and parent communication channels under a single secure portal.

How does online fee collection work?

The system generates student invoices based on classroom tuition, transport, or lab rates, and sends digital reminders to parents. Parents can pay bills securely via debit cards, credit cards, or direct ACH transfers, which reconciles student accounts instantly.

Can we import our existing student rosters?

Yes. Our engineering team handles full data migration from spreadsheet databases or older legacy programs, verifying that student files, household records, fee logs, and grading histories are successfully transferred with zero downtime.

Is the student directory safe and compliant?

Yes. Our databases operate on secure AWS cloud servers featuring AES-256 encryption. We utilize role-based access permissions so that student records, grades, and household data can only be accessed by authorized staff members.

Do you support multi-site school districts?

Yes. Our multi-campus District mode features dropdown filtering switchers that allow superintendents to oversee registries, schedules, and financial reports globally, while campus principals manage their own school dashboard view.

Do you offer white-label school ERP software for resellers?

Yes. We offer a comprehensive partner reseller program. IT consultants, local software vendors, or startup founders can deploy our campus ERP system as a fully branded white-label school ERP software, utilizing custom domains, branding themes, and compiled mobile apps.
