FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Senior Care ERP setup.

Is this system fully white-labeled?

Yes. The platform is configured, hosted, and launched under your own agency domain (e.g., portal.youragency.com). All caregiver interfaces, emails, notifications, and client portals feature your company branding, logos, and styling guidelines.

Is the database HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Client health logs, clinical details, and visit files are stored using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. We implement strict access controls, secure TLS connections, and data isolation protocols to align with HIPAA data safety requirements.

How does Electronic Visit Verification (EVV) compliance work?

The system uses GPS tracking at the exact moment of check-in and check-out on the caregiver's mobile web portal, logging location coordinate stamps, timestamp verification, and client verification signatures, fully satisfying the requirements of the Cures Act.
