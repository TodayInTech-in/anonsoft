FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common integration questions regarding CareNav ERP.

Is this platform fully white-labeled?

Yes. The portal launches on your own custom domain (e.g. portal.youragency.com). Caregiver check-ins, notifications, invoices, and family interfaces feature your agency branding, styles, and logos.

Is the database HIPAA compliant?

Yes. All clinical notes, ADL logs, and patient records are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3, maintaining full data protection compliance.

How does GPS visit verification work?

When check-in or check-out is pressed on the caregiver's mobile browser, the portal records GPS coordinates, timestamps, and caregiver authentication, generating audit-proof verification records.
