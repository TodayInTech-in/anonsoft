The Problem

Small and mid-sized businesses struggle to keep stock levels, invoicing, and sales reporting in sync. Most rely on spreadsheets or disconnected tools that lead to stock-outs, billing errors, and zero visibility into what's actually selling. Off-the-shelf SaaS products are rigid, expensive per seat, and can't be rebranded — making them a non-starter for agencies and resellers who want to offer billing software under their own brand.

  • Stock counts and sales data live in separate, unsynced spreadsheets
  • Manual invoicing leads to errors and slow billing
  • No real-time visibility into best-sellers or low-stock items
  • Per-seat SaaS pricing becomes expensive as teams grow
  • No way to rebrand existing tools for resale to clients

Our Solution

We built a premium white-label inventory software and billing platform that unifies stock tracking, invoicing, and sales analytics in one dashboard. Every element — logo, colors, domain, and branding — is configurable, so businesses and resellers can deploy it as their own product. The system handles GST-compliant billing, real-time stock tracking, multi-user roles, and detailed reporting out of the box.

  • Real-time multi-warehouse inventory software custom setups with cross-location transfers
  • Professional, GST-compliant invoice & quotation generation
  • Fully white-label — custom branding, colors, and domain
  • Multi-user access with role-based permissions
  • Sales, purchase, and tax reports with exportable data
  • Customer and supplier management built in
  • Ready to deploy for any business or reseller

The Result

The platform gave businesses a single source of truth for stock and billing, eliminating manual reconciliation and slashing invoicing time. Its white-label architecture turned it into a ready-to-resell product, opening a new revenue stream for agencies and distributors who deploy it under their own brand.

  • Real-time stock and sales synced in one dashboard
  • GST-ready invoicing in a few clicks
  • 100% white-label — deploy under any brand
  • Multi-user role-based access for whole teams
  • Reseller-ready — a new revenue stream for partners
