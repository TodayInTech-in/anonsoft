The Problem

Small and medium-sized businesses were struggling with fragmented marketing tools. They were using separate platforms for WhatsApp messaging, email campaigns, and customer support — leading to data silos, inconsistent messaging, and wasted time switching between tools. Most affordable solutions lacked AI capabilities, and enterprise tools were too expensive for growing businesses.

  • Marketing spread across 4+ disconnected tools
  • No unified view of customer interactions
  • Manual lead follow-ups resulting in lost opportunities
  • No AI-powered automation for customer engagement

Our Solution

We built NexBotix — a unified multi-channel marketing platform that combines WhatsApp Business API integration, professional email campaigns with SMTP support, and AI-powered website chatbots into a single dashboard. The platform includes contact management, campaign analytics, automated follow-ups, and a drag-and-drop chatbot builder.

  • WhatsApp Business API integration for bulk messaging and automation
  • Professional email campaign builder with templates and analytics
  • AI-powered website chatbot with custom training
  • Unified contact management and CRM
  • Campaign performance analytics and reporting
  • Automated lead scoring and follow-up sequences

The Result

NexBotix is now used by multiple businesses to manage their entire marketing operations from one platform. Clients have reported significant improvements in lead conversion, response times, and overall marketing efficiency.

  • 3x increase in lead conversion rates
  • 70% reduction in response time with AI chatbot
  • 85% less time spent managing marketing tools
  • 40% higher email open rates with optimized campaigns
  • Single dashboard replacing 4+ separate tools
