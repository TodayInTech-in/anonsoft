The Problem

The client, a healthcare startup in the Philippines, needed a full-featured telehealth platform to connect patients with doctors remotely. Existing solutions were either too expensive to license or lacked critical features like HIPAA-compliant video calls, e-prescriptions, and integration with local healthcare systems. They needed a custom platform that could be launched quickly and scaled as the business grew.

  • No affordable HIPAA-compliant telehealth solution available
  • Manual appointment booking via phone calls
  • Paper-based prescriptions and patient records
  • No way for patients to consult doctors remotely

Our Solution

We built Mednowna from the ground up — a comprehensive telehealth platform with secure HD video consultations, digital appointment scheduling, electronic health records, e-prescriptions with pharmacy integration, and a cross-platform mobile app. The entire system was designed to be HIPAA-compliant with end-to-end encryption and role-based access controls.

  • Secure WebRTC-based video consultations with adaptive bitrate
  • Digital appointment scheduling with automated reminders
  • Electronic health records and patient history management
  • E-prescription system with pharmacy network integration
  • Cross-platform apps for iOS, Android, and web
  • HIPAA-compliant architecture with AES-256 encryption

The Result

Mednowna launched successfully and quickly became the client's primary healthcare delivery channel. The platform eliminated paper-based workflows, reduced appointment no-shows, and enabled the client to serve patients across the country rather than just within clinic proximity.

  • 80% reduction in appointment no-shows
  • 3x increase in daily patient consultations
  • 100% paperless prescription and record management
  • 4 weeks from kickoff to MVP launch
  • Zero data breaches — fully HIPAA compliant

HIPAA Compliance, BAA Signing, & Security

As an experienced HIPAA-compliant telemedicine app developer, we build secure, zero-trust architectures to protect Patient Health Information (PHI) at every layer. Our virtual care solutions are ready for security audits and offer full BAA support:

BAA Signing Ready

We sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with clinics and startups, legally underwriting our adherence to HIPAA safeguards throughout design, hosting, and maintenance cycles.

Zero-Knowledge Encryption

We implement double-layer encryption (AES-256 for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit) combined with Cloud KMS envelope decryption to prevent unauthorized PHI exposure.

Auditable Access Logs

Every system call, clinical record access, and credential verification is written to an immutable audit trail, complete with automatic anomaly detection triggers.
