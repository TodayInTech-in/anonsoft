Radio Stiming — Live Radio Streaming Platform

A full-featured live radio streaming platform with show scheduling, DJ profiles, song request system, and real-time listener engagement — serving 100,000+ monthly listeners across web and mobile.

View Live Demo Build Something Similar

The Problem

The client operated a growing radio station with 5+ years on air but had no online presence. Their entire operation — show scheduling, listener engagement, song requests, and host profiles — was managed manually through spreadsheets and phone calls. They were losing listeners to digital-first competitors and had no way to reach audiences beyond their FM signal range.

  • No live streaming or web presence
  • Manual show scheduling via spreadsheets
  • No listener engagement or song request system
  • Limited reach — FM only, no global audience

Our Solution

We designed and built a complete digital radio station platform from the ground up. The system includes real-time audio streaming, an automated show schedule with live status indicators, detailed DJ profiles, a listener song request system, a news and blog section, and a fully responsive mobile-first design — all wrapped in a premium dark-gold visual identity that matches the station's brand.

  • Real-time audio streaming with persistent player
  • Automated show scheduling with live/upcoming indicators
  • Interactive song request and dedication system
  • DJ profile pages with bios and show history
  • News and blog management system
  • Mobile-first responsive design

The Result

Within 3 months of launch, the station expanded its reach from a local FM audience to a global listener base. The platform now serves 100,000+ monthly listeners, hosts 14 live shows weekly with 6 expert DJs, and has completely eliminated manual scheduling overhead.

  • 100,000+ monthly listeners (up from ~15,000 FM-only)
  • 6x increase in audience reach
  • 90% reduction in administrative overhead
  • 500+ song requests processed weekly
  • 24/7 automated live streaming with zero downtime
