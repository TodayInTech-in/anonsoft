The Problem

When shopping for prefab home configurator websites and sustainable timber modular housing in Germany, prospective homebuilders faced overwhelming complexity comparing square footage, energy standards (KfW 40), roof designs, and turnkey package costs. The client needed an inspiring platform to guide families from concept to consultation.

  • Overwhelming construction jargon confusing prospective first-time homebuilders
  • Lack of interactive floorplan visualization and modular expansion previews
  • Opaque pricing models preventing qualified inbound inquiries
  • Strict German construction regulation transparency requirements

Our Solution

We engineered a modern sustainable construction portal development platform featuring interactive floorplan visualizers, model comparison tools, energy subsidy calculators, and a seamless consultation booking funnel.

  • Interactive modular house configurator with real-time floorplan exploration
  • German KfW energy efficiency and government subsidy estimation tool
  • Transparent base-pricing and optional finish selection (solar panels, heat pumps, green roofs)
  • Virtual 3D model galleries and video walk-through tours
  • Direct scheduling with regional construction consultants and showroom managers

The Result

EasyFamily Haus became one of Germany's most digitally progressive prefabricated home providers, generating unprecedented high-value homeowner inquiries.

  • +210% surge in qualified showroom appointment bookings
  • 5.4 minutes average session duration exploring interactive floorplans
  • €12M+ in estimated home construction contracts generated via the platform
  • Top 3 organic ranking in Germany for eco-friendly modular home keywords
