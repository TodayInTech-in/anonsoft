Restaurant Management Software FAQs

How long does the setup process take?

Our rapid deployment ensures your restaurant portal, menu configurations, and POS settings are active within 3 to 5 business days. Additional branding for mobile apps takes about 7 to 10 days for store approvals.

Is it fully white-label?

Yes, all plans under our Enterprise tier support complete custom branding. This includes custom domains, your logo on receipts, branded staff screens, and customer-facing websites/mobile apps without any TodayInTech watermark.

Does the POS work offline if the internet drops?

Absolutely. The POS app caches menu data locally and handles order billing offline. Once internet connectivity is restored, all offline transactions, sales stats, and ingredient updates are automatically synchronized to the cloud.

Can I link receipt printers and cash drawers?

Yes. Our software integrates seamlessly with standard thermal receipt printers (Epson, Star Micronics) via LAN, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth. It also supports cash drawer triggers and barcode scanning devices.

How does the delivery tracking work for customers?

Once an order is dispatched, customers receive a live tracking link via SMS or WhatsApp. They can view the rider's real-time GPS location on a map, estimated arrival time, and order status milestones — all from a branded, no-app-required tracking page hosted on your own domain.

Can we integrate with Zomato, Swiggy, or other platforms?

Yes. Our order aggregation module pulls incoming orders from Zomato, Swiggy, and other supported platforms directly into the POS and kitchen display, eliminating the need to manage separate devices. Each platform integration is available as an add-on at $99 per platform.
