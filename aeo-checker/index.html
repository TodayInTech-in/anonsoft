          The Next Era of Search
          
What is AI Engine Optimization (AEO)?

          

            Over 40% of search queries now occur on conversational AI platforms. If your site lacks structured AI files like /llms.txt, AI models hallucinate or completely omit your brand from recommendations.
          

        


        

          

            

            
Generative Engine Discovery

            
AI models do not parse bloated HTML, JavaScript bundles, or cookie popups. They seek token-efficient, factual Markdown files that directly feed their attention context.

          

          

            

            
Perplexity & ChatGPT Citations

            
Having a validated llms.txt dramatically increases the probability that ChatGPT Search and Perplexity will cite your SaaS, pricing, and features as verified source links.

          

          

            

            
Autonomous Agent Ready

            
With llms-full.txt and Model Context Protocol (MCP), AI developer agents (like Claude Desktop, Cursor, and OpenCode) can immediately execute workflows with your API.

          Common Questions
          
AEO & LLMS.txt FAQs

        


        

          

            
              What is the difference between an EHR and an EMR vs AI Indexing?
              +
            
            

              
Just as EHR systems standardize healthcare data exchange via FHIR APIs, /llms.txt standardizes web data exchange for Large Language Models. Without it, AI agents struggle to accurately understand your company's offerings.

            

          


          

            
              Where should llms.txt be placed on my website?
              +
            
            

              
The standard specification mandates placing the file at the exact root of your domain: https://yourdomain.com/llms.txt and the full documentation knowledge base at https://yourdomain.com/llms-full.txt.

            

          


          

            
              Does having an llms.txt hurt traditional SEO?
              +
            
            

              
Not at all. In fact, Google Search and Google Gemini bot both utilize llms.txt to better understand site architecture. It complements your sitemap.xml and robots.txt without creating duplicate content issues.
