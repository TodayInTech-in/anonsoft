High-Level System Architecture of an Agentic AI Medical Scribe

Building a commercial-grade, HIPAA-compliant ambient scribe requires a 4-tier microservices architecture:

Tier 1: Ambient Audio Streaming & Diarization Engine

The client app (React Native mobile or web widget) captures multi-channel ambient audio during clinical encounters. Audio frames are streamed via secure WebSockets (WSS) using TLS 1.3 encryption to a speech processing pipeline (e.g., Deepgram Nova-3 Medical or Whisper Large v3).

Speaker Diarization: The engine separates speaker channels into Doctor, Patient, and Family Member with sub-second latency.

Tier 2: Agentic LLM Orchestrator & Clinical Entity Extractor

Once transcript chunks are generated, an Agentic AI orchestrator (built on LangGraph or custom Python microservices) routes data through specialized clinical prompt pipelines:

  • Medical Entity Recognition (NER): Detects symptoms, medications, dosages, lab values, and past medical history.
  • ICD-10 & CPT Coding Suggestion Engine: Maps documented diagnoses to official billing codes.
  • SOAP Note Generator: Structurally formats raw conversation into Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan notes aligned with specialty-specific clinical templates.

Tier 3: SMART-on-FHIR EMR Integration & Document Reference Push

After the physician reviews and approves the drafted note in the web/mobile interface, the AI Scribe engine formats the note into an HL7 FHIR R4 DocumentReference resource and pushes it directly into the practice EMR (Epic, Cerner, Athenahealth, eClinicalWorks).

HIPAA Compliance & Security Checklist for AI Scribes

To avoid severe regulatory penalties under HHS and FTC rules, every Agentic AI Scribe must implement:

  • Zero Data Retention (ZDR) LLM Agreements: Ensure LLM vendors (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Vertex AI) sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with explicit zero-retention policies (no training on PHI).
  • PHI Scrubbing & De-Identification: Strip direct identifiers (names, SSNs, phone numbers) before sending transcripts to processing pipelines.
  • Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Enforce strict OAuth 2.0 / OIDC authentication with multi-factor authentication (MFA) for every clinician session.
  • Cryptographic Audit Trails: Log all access attempts, audio processing jobs, note edits, and EMR pushes in an immutable, encrypted database.
