DrCare247 Alternatives: Top 5 White-Label Telehealth Platforms in 2026

Selecting the right white-label telehealth platform is a high-stakes decision for clinics, healthcare agencies, and digital health startups. DrCare247 is a popular cloud-based solution, but recurring monthly licensing fees and a lack of source code ownership drive many businesses to search for alternatives. Here, we evaluate the top 5 DrCare247 alternatives for custom, HIPAA-compliant patient care platforms in 2026.

Why Look for DrCare247 Alternatives?

DrCare247 is an established SaaS platform that provides out-of-the-box telehealth capabilities, clinical charts, and remote patient monitoring. While it is useful for clinics that want a quick setup without worrying about code, growing medical brands and digital health startups often face significant limitations:

  • Zero Code Ownership: With DrCare247, you lease the software. You cannot host the code on your own cloud servers, export the fully customized database schema, or transfer the platform to another team if you want to sell the business.
  • Recurring Licensing Costs: Charging on a monthly subscription fee model per-provider can quickly become expensive as your healthcare business grows. An agency with 50+ providers can end up paying thousands of dollars monthly for leased software.
  • Rigid Customization Limitations: Although SaaS platforms are configurable, they cannot support proprietary clinical workflows, custom third-party integrations, or specialized database engines that differ from their core software setup.
  • Onboarding Fees and Long Startup Setup: Rebranding and setting up white-label mobile apps and domain mappings on their cloud infrastructure often comes with steep setup fees and delays.
The 2026 Market Shift: Clinical software buyers are moving away from restrictive SaaS contracts. They want custom-engineered applications with pre-built modules for standard features (like calendar scheduling and video connections) but require full intellectual property ownership.

2. Healee — Best for Fast Patient Portal Setup

Healee is a highly configurable, white-label SaaS telehealth platform. It is an excellent choice for medical groups that want a functional patient booking page, intake forms, and video conferencing tools up and running in less than a month. However, because it is leased, you cannot export the code to host it on your own server, and you must pay recurring per-provider monthly licensing fees as your clinic expands.

3. OpenLoop Health — Best for Telehealth + Clinician Staffing

If your primary challenge is finding licensed clinicians to staff your telemedicine platform, OpenLoop is a robust choice. They offer both a HIPAA-compliant virtual care platform and a massive network of multi-state licensed doctors, nurses, and specialists ready to plug into your service. This combined software-and-staffing approach is great for scale, though the custom branding control of the software itself is restricted compared to a custom build.

4. ContinuousCare — Best for Virtual Practice Management

ContinuousCare provides small-to-medium healthcare clinics with virtual practice portals, mobile apps, and booking systems. It offers an easy-to-use patient engagement layer that includes text consultations and chronic disease trackers. While highly accessible for single practices, it lacks the scalability and custom database API integrations required by larger enterprise digital health brands.

Summary: How to Choose Your Telehealth Partner

Use the following criteria to guide your platform decision:

  • Choose DrCare247 if: You want a cloud-ready SaaS setup with standard patient monitoring, do not require custom features, and are comfortable with monthly licensing fees per provider.
  • Choose Healee if: You need an instant patient portal with flexible intake forms and do not need to own the underlying intellectual property.
  • Choose OpenLoop if: You need a white-label system and need access to their clinical staffing network to handle patient volumes.
  • Choose TodayInTech if: You want a fully customized, HIPAA-compliant telehealth platform launched in 4-8 weeks, want complete code ownership to eliminate recurring platform fees, and value reviewing a working prototype before making a financial commitment.
