Tebra Alternatives: Top 5 EHR & Practice Management Platforms (2026)

Are you looking to replace Tebra (formerly Kareo and PatientPop) with a more flexible or cost-effective healthcare platform? While Tebra combines patient growth and clinical workflows into a single system, medical practice administrators frequently struggle with escalating monthly fees, limited customization options, and rigid software boundaries. Here is our expert guide to the top 5 Tebra alternatives in 2026.

Why Practice Owners Are Replacing Tebra in 2026

Tebra was formed through the merger of Kareo (EHR & billing) and PatientPop (practice growth & SEO). While the bundled package appeals to newly opened practices, growing medical groups and specialty clinics hit several friction points as their clinical team expands:

  • High Bundled Subscription Rates: Monthly per-provider fees add up quickly when adding medical assistants, nurse practitioners, and multi-location clinicians.
  • Restricted Patient Portals: Tebra's standard patient interface leaves limited room for custom branding, specialized clinical intake questionnaires, or custom telehealth workflows.
  • Opaque Revenue Cycle Management Costs: Tebra RCM services take percentage cuts of billing revenue, which can become prohibitively expensive for high-volume practices.
  • No IP Ownership: Practice owners pay tens of thousands of dollars annually without building proprietary software equity for their business.

2. SimplePractice — Ideal for Solo Behavioral & Allied Health Clinicians

If you run an independent therapy, counseling, or solo wellness practice, SimplePractice provides a clean, easy-to-use software suite for booking appointments, taking clinical notes, and billing insurance. However, it lacks the multi-provider enterprise controls required by growing multi-specialty medical practices.

3. DrChrono — Best for iPad-First Mobile Medical Charting

DrChrono was built from the ground up for iOS and iPad devices, offering customizable clinical chart templates and integrated e-prescribing. It is a solid choice for physicians who prefer mobile point-of-care charting, though monthly costs scale significantly with additional providers.

4. Athenahealth — Enterprise-Grade Revenue Cycle Management

For large medical groups and hospital-affiliated clinics, Athenahealth is an industry benchmark for RCM and medical billing. Athenahealth operates on a percentage-of-collections model, making it powerful for complex billing claims, though its interface can feel rigid for smaller practice teams.

Which Tebra Alternative Is Right for You?

Your choice depends on your practice size, customization needs, and growth strategy:

  • Choose SimplePractice if: You are a solo therapist or counselor seeking low-cost, ready-made practice management software.
  • Choose DrChrono if: You rely heavily on iPad mobile charting and standard EHR templates.
  • Choose Athenahealth if: You operate a high-volume multi-specialty medical center needing complex enterprise billing services.
