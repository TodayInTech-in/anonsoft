5 Key Criteria When Selecting a Healthcare Development Partner in 2026

  1. Mandatory Business Associate Agreement (BAA): Never work with a developer unwilling to sign a legally binding BAA prior to code deployment.
  2. TEFCA & FHIR R4/R5 Interoperability: Modern US healthcare platforms must interoperate with major EHR systems like Epic, Cerner, and Athenahealth using standardized HL7 FHIR APIs.
  3. Agentic AI Integration Readiness: Ensure your partner can deploy autonomous AI scribes and prior-authorization agents securely without leaking PHI to public LLMs.
  4. Launch Timeline vs. Cost Tradeoff: Evaluate whether building from scratch ($100k+, 6+ months) or customizing a white-label platform ($15k-$30k, 4-8 weeks) aligns better with your budget and go-to-market plan.
  5. Full IP & Code Ownership: Verify that you retain 100% ownership of source code, custom algorithms, and database schemas upon completion.
