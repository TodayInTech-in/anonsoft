Healee Alternatives: Top 5 Telehealth & Patient Portals in 2026

Healee offers a highly configurable, white-label SaaS telehealth platform designed for medical groups and clinics. It provides patient scheduling, secure messaging, video consultations, and basic billing under one subscription. However, for growing digital health startups and enterprise medical networks, Healee's leased SaaS model, lack of source code ownership, and scaling seat licenses can eventually become cost-prohibitive blockers. Here is an in-depth comparison of the top 5 Healee alternatives in 2026.

Why Digital Health Builders Look for Healee Alternatives

Healee is an excellent solution for medical practices that want to spin up a branded patient booking page and video clinic without investing in custom engineering. However, as organizations scale, three key challenges usually prompt them to seek a Healee competitor:

  • Leased SaaS Restrictions (No IP Ownership): Because Healee is a software-as-a-service provider, you do not own the source code, custom schemas, or underlying infrastructure. If you want to pitch to venture capitalists, secure institutional buyers, or execute a merger, not owning your product's intellectual property is a major valuation penalty.
  • Scaling seat licenses: As your clinical network expands, Healee's per-provider monthly subscription fees can escalate dramatically. What was affordable for a clinic of 5 doctors becomes a major financial drain when scaling to 100+ providers or incorporating third-party wellness coaches.
  • Rigid Integration Boundaries: While Healee offers custom configuration options and API endpoints, you are restricted to their pre-built layout patterns and data structures. Building highly customized features, such as custom IoT sensor data pipelines, complex clinical trials algorithms, or bespoke UX screens for elderly care, is frequently impossible.
The 2026 Reality: Many healthcare groups start on leased SaaS platforms to validate their business model, only to realize that custom software is essential to build enterprise value, integrate custom data systems, and control long-term operating costs.

2. SimplePractice — Best for Independent Solo Practitioners

For independent therapists, counselors, and solo practitioners who do not need a custom brand or complex EHR integrations, SimplePractice is the gold standard. It provides ready-made appointment scheduling, paperless intakes, telehealth sessions, and insurance billing. It is highly streamlined but cannot be customized to look like your own proprietary software platform.

3. Doxy.me — Best for Simplicity and Rapid Video Setup

If you already have a functional EHR or practice management system and simply need a secure, HIPAA-compliant video link to conduct patient visits, Doxy.me is a perfect fit. It is browser-based, meaning patients do not need to download an app or create an account to start a visit. However, it lacks the deep booking workflows and customized patient portals that Healee and TodayInTech support.

4. Carepatron — Best for Collaborative Clinical Teams on a Budget

Carepatron is a modern, collaborative workspace for healthcare practitioners. It offers scheduling, progress notes, invoicing, and patient portal features. With its flexible, freemium tier and intuitive design, it is a great fit for small teams of clinicians looking to manage administrative tasks online without investing in custom software or complex enterprise configurations.

Summary: How to Choose Your Telehealth Partner

Select your partner based on your long-term business goals:

  • Choose Healee if: You want a fully-configured patient portal and scheduling app launched quickly, do not want to manage any custom software code, and are happy with a recurring licensing model.
  • Choose SimplePractice if: You are a solo clinician or therapist looking for an all-in-one practice management tool with no technical customization requirements.
  • Choose Doxy.me if: Your only requirement is a reliable, secure video conferencing URL for clinical consultations.
  • Choose TodayInTech if: You want a unique, brand-aligned patient portal, need custom integrations with specialized medical systems, want to completely eliminate recurring seat licensing fees, and want to own the intellectual property of your software.
