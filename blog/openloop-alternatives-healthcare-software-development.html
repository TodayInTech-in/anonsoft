OpenLoop Alternatives: Top 5 B2B Telehealth Infrastructure Platforms (2026)

Deploying a compliant virtual care service requires a careful balance of technology and clinical staffing. OpenLoop Health has emerged as a key player in clinical networks and multi-state provider staffing. However, if you are looking to retain complete control over your technology stack, customize clinical workflows, or avoid ongoing licensing fees, here is an in-depth guide to the top OpenLoop alternatives in 2026.

Why Digital Health Founders Look for OpenLoop Alternatives

OpenLoop Health is well-regarded for its fractional clinician staffing and licensing services, offering a ready-made clinical network alongside telehealth software. While this setup works for teams needing rapid plug-and-play provider access, it introduces constraints that B2B healthcare companies and clinical groups frequently try to avoid:

  • Locked-in Technology: OpenLoop's software is tightly bound to their staffing infrastructure. Customizing specialized clinical EHR records, integrating third-party wearables, or designing unique patient intake flows can be difficult because you do not own the source code.
  • Provider Seat & Licensing Fees: Scaling a virtual clinic on a pre-built platform means paying recurring platform fees per provider or per consultation. As patient volumes grow, these licensing fees erode profit margins.
  • IP Ownership Gaps: To attract venture capital funding or prepare for acquisition, digital health startups need to prove they own their primary IP. With a leased platform, you have zero technology assets to show investors.

2. Wheel — The Industry Giant for API-Based Staffing

Wheel is a prominent name in virtual care infrastructure. They provide a massive, pre-vetted clinician network alongside APIs to power virtual visits. Unlike OpenLoop, Wheel focuses heavily on its API layer, allowing developers to embed clinical capacity directly into their own systems. However, utilizing Wheel still requires substantial custom software development to build the front-end patient portals and database bridges, and you remain dependent on their API uptime and pricing rules.

3. Medcase — Global Multi-Specialty Clinical Network

Medcase specializes in providing access to global medical experts and healthcare professionals. Their primary focus is on connecting digital health platforms, AI developers, and clinical trials with the exact clinical talent they need. While they offer white-label portals for clinical reviews, they are not designed to serve as a fully customizable patient-facing telehealth app. They are best used to staff complex projects rather than build custom software assets.

4. Bask Health — The Turnkey D2C Telehealth Platform

Bask Health is a SaaS platform optimized for direct-to-consumer digital health brands. They offer pre-built landing pages, physician networks, and pharmacy integrations. If you are launching a standard wellness subscription and do not need complex medical integrations or custom WebGL features, Bask Health provides a fast path to launch. However, you will pay ongoing platform commissions and have no technology assets to own.

Summary: Which OpenLoop Competitor is Right for Your Team?

  • Choose OpenLoop if: You need an all-in-one partner to handle licensing, credentialing, and clinician staffing, and are comfortable using their standard software platform.
  • Choose Wheel if: You already have a dev team building a portal and simply need high-quality clinician staffing APIs to plug into it.
  • Choose Medcase if: Your project requires highly specialized, global medical talent, particularly for clinical trials or AI data training.
  • Choose Bask Health if: You are launching a straightforward D2C prescription brand and want a fast, standard SaaS setup.
  • Choose TodayInTech if: You want a specialized custom telehealth platform tailored to your exact clinical workflow, need to eliminate technical risk with a free working prototype, and want 100% IP ownership to avoid platform licensing fees and build a valuable B2B software asset.
