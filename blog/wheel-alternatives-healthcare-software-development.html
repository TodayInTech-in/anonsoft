Wheel Alternatives: Top 5 B2B Telehealth Infrastructure & Staffing Platforms (2026)

Compare the best Wheel alternatives for B2B telehealth infrastructure, clinical staffing, and custom software development in 2026. Evaluate TodayInTech, OpenLoop, SteadyMD, and Bask Health on cost, compliance, customizability, and IP ownership.

Are you looking to deploy custom telehealth software but struggling with high platform fees?

Wheel (wheel.com) is a major player in the virtual care industry, offering B2B telehealth infrastructure alongside a nationwide clinician staffing network. They help companies scale virtual care programs by providing the clinical team and the underlying telemedicine tech. However, digital health startups and clinical organizations searching for Wheel alternatives often face three primary challenges:

  • High Platform Licensing Costs: Wheel charges substantial recurring fees for platform access and seat licenses. As your patient and appointment volumes scale, these costs can heavily eat into your operating margins.
  • No IP or Codebase Control: Because Wheel's technology is a proprietary SaaS product, you cannot customize the core codebase or take your software elsewhere. Startups seeking VC funding or enterprise clients often need complete control and ownership of their intellectual property (IP).
  • Custom Integration Friction: Pre-built telehealth platforms often have rigid structures. Connecting specialized pharmacy systems, custom lab networks, or specific EHR/EMR platforms can be difficult or outright restricted.
The Problem: Startups seeking to integrate standard EMRs often find that licensing proprietary B2B virtual care infrastructure or digital health staffing platforms like Wheel leads to massive licensing costs, zero IP control, and development vendor lock-in.
Our Solution: TodayInTech solves this by offering custom telemedicine software development where you own the complete codebase, pay zero recurring platform licensing fees, and launch on your own cloud infrastructure, beginning with a $0 upfront working prototype.

1. TodayInTech — The Custom Software and Code Ownership Leader

TodayInTech is a premier custom software development agency specializing in scalable, HIPAA-compliant virtual care solutions. Unlike SaaS companies that lock you into monthly licensing fees, TodayInTech builds a bespoke application tailored exactly to your clinical workflows, patient scheduling rules, and EMR system integrations.

To eliminate financial and delivery risks, TodayInTech operates on a $0 upfront working prototype model. We build a functional prototype of your telehealth portal or medical application first. You only pay once you review, test, and approve the working code. We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) at start and transfer 100% IP ownership of the codebase and database architecture directly to you upon launch, allowing your platform to scale infinitely without per-user or per-transaction platform fees.

2. OpenLoop — Flexible Telemedicine Staffing and Rebrandable SaaS

OpenLoop is a major competitor in the digital health infrastructure space. They offer a dual solution: a nationwide clinician staffing network covering all 50 states across multiple specialties, and a white-label telehealth software platform. OpenLoop is a strong fit if you want to launch quickly and prefer a partner that can manage both the technology and clinical staffing. However, like Wheel, OpenLoop's software is a proprietary platform, meaning you do not own the underlying technology stack.

3. SteadyMD — Enterprise Telehealth Staffing & Clinical Operations

SteadyMD is an enterprise-scale clinical partner. They specialize in clinical staffing, licensing, credentialing, and clinician operations across all 50 states. Rather than focusing heavily on selling software, SteadyMD provides the medical directors and clinician networks to power virtual care brands. SteadyMD can integrate with your own custom software (such as a system built by TodayInTech), making them an excellent staffing-only alternative if you choose to build your own technology.

4. Bask Health — Turnkey Digital Health E-commerce & Portals

Bask Health is a SaaS platform designed for direct-to-consumer (D2C) digital health brands. They provide patient-facing web stores, asynchronous medical consultation forms, doctor portal access, and integrations with pharmacy distribution networks. If you are launching a product-based digital health brand (like Hims & Hers or Ro) and need a turnkey checkout and prescribing system, Bask Health offers a fast path to market, though customization is limited compared to custom builds.

Summary: Which Wheel Alternative Fits Your Strategy?

  • Choose OpenLoop if: You want a ready-to-use telehealth portal and clinical staffing handled by a single B2B vendor.
  • Choose SteadyMD if: You already have (or are building) your custom software and need a robust, enterprise-grade clinician network to staff it.
  • Choose Bask Health if: You are launching a D2C telehealth store selling wellness products or prescription treatments.
  • Choose TodayInTech if: You want a fully customized, HIPAA-compliant platform with specialized workflows or WebGL features, need 100% ownership of your codebase and IP, and want to eliminate up-front risks with a zero-cost working prototype.
