Healthie Alternatives: Top 5 Telehealth & EHR Platforms in 2026

Are you looking to deploy a branded digital health platform without getting locked into expensive per-provider monthly subscription fees? Healthie is a widely used API-first EHR and scheduling platform for digital health companies. However, as medical startups and clinical practices scale to tens or hundreds of providers, Healthie's per-seat SaaS costs, strict feature limits, and lack of source code ownership force many teams to evaluate top Healthie alternatives in 2026.

Why Digital Health Founders are Seeking Healthie Alternatives

Healthie gained popularity by providing an API-first backend for telehealth scheduling, patient intake, charting, and billing. While it allows startups to launch quickly without building an EHR from scratch, growing companies encounter significant roadblocks:

  • Escalating Provider Licensing Fees: Healthie charges monthly fees ranging from $149 to $499+ per provider. When scaling a tele-wellness platform or multi-state clinical group with 50 to 200+ providers, annual software licensing expenses can easily cross six figures every year.
  • Zero Source Code or IP Ownership: Because Healthie is a multi-tenant SaaS, you are renting access to their servers. Investors in venture-backed healthcare platforms increasingly demand proprietary IP ownership rather than reliance on third-party SaaS infrastructure.
  • API Rate Limits and Schema Constraints: While Healthie offers a GraphQL API, complex clinical workflows—such as real-time continuous glucose monitor (CGM) analytics, automated prior authorizations, or custom multi-tier commission structures—are constrained by Healthie's database architecture.
Key 2026 Insight: Digital health startups that build custom healthcare platforms retain 100% of their company valuation equity, avoid per-provider monthly seat taxes, and control their patient data pipeline natively.

2. Practice Better — Top Alternative for Nutrition & Holistic Health

Practice Better is a popular SaaS portal designed specifically for nutritionists, health coaches, and holistic healthcare providers. It provides food journals, supplement recommendations, client booking, and payment processing out of the box. While easy to use, it does not support custom medical billing or deep EHR integrations.

3. SimplePractice — Ideal for Solo Behavioral Health Professionals

For independent therapists and mental health counselors, SimplePractice offers automated billing, paperless intake forms, and telehealth video sessions. It is budget-friendly for solo providers, but lacks white-label flexibility and enterprise multi-location management.

4. Doxy.me — Best Low-Cost Option for Simple Video Telehealth

Doxy.me focuses strictly on simple, browser-based HIPAA-compliant video consultations. It requires no software installation for patients. However, it is not a full EHR and does not include comprehensive patient charting, e-Prescribing, or custom lab workflows.
