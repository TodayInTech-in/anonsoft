Bask Health Alternatives: Top 5 White-Label Telehealth Platforms (2026)

Compare the best Bask Health alternatives for B2B telehealth software, clinical portals, and custom web applications in 2026. Evaluate TodayInTech, Healee, OpenLoop, and DrCare247 on licensing costs, customization limits, and IP ownership.

Are you looking to deploy a white-label telehealth platform but worried about proprietary software lock-in?

Bask Health (bask.health) is widely regarded as a convenient option in the digital health space, acting as an all-in-one patient intake and prescription checkout engine. Frequently described as the Shopify of telehealth, it enables founders to build D2C health brands using pre-configured doctor portals, asynchronous intake questionnaires, and direct connections to compounding pharmacies. However, startups seeking to scale long-term and establish strict enterprise value often search for a Bask Health competitor due to the following critical limitations:

  • Zero Code Ownership (Platform Lock-in): Bask Health is a closed SaaS system. You cannot download your source code, customize database queries, or host the system on your own servers. If you decide to transition to another vendor, migrating your historical patient charts and customized intake logic can be highly challenging.
  • Scaling Costs & Transaction Fees: Standard telehealth SaaS platforms operate on recurring subscription fees coupled with transaction markup fees. As your patient base grows from hundreds to thousands of consultations per month, these overheads quickly drain your profit margins.
  • Rigid Customization Limitations: While its drag-and-drop form builder works well for standard medical questionnaires, adding advanced features such as interactive 3D WebGL anatomical customizers, custom wearable device data streams (IoMT), or multi-branch clinics with complex routing rules is not supported.
The Problem: Founders looking to launch a digital health brand often discover that using a B2B SaaS platform like Bask Health forces them into proprietary platform lock-in, recurring transaction fees, and zero codebase IP ownership.
Our Solution: TodayInTech provides a powerful alternative by offering custom telemedicine software development with immediate, 100% intellectual property ownership, zero ongoing software licensing costs, and a $0 upfront working prototype to prove compliance and workflow capabilities.

2. Healee — Highly Configurable Patient Portals

Healee is an excellent Bask Health alternative if you want to avoid starting from scratch but need more configuration options than Bask offers. It provides solid scheduling tools, clinical forms, and asynchronous telehealth modules. It is widely used by mid-size healthcare groups who want a customized patient portal. However, like other SaaS models, you are still renting their software and do not own the source code.

3. OpenLoop — Combined Clinician Staffing and SaaS

OpenLoop is a major competitor in the digital health infrastructure space. They offer a dual solution: a nationwide clinician staffing network covering all 50 states across multiple specialties, and a white-label telehealth software platform. OpenLoop is a strong fit if you want to launch quickly and prefer a partner that can manage both the technology and clinical staffing. However, like Wheel and Bask Health, OpenLoop's software is a proprietary platform, meaning you do not own the underlying technology stack.

4. DrCare247 — Traditional White-Label Telemedicine

DrCare247 is a long-standing white-label telemedicine platform provider. They offer standard Patient/Provider/Admin portals, electronic prescriptions, scheduling, and video consulting. If you are a traditional clinic looking to add basic digital appointment capabilities to your existing physical practice, DrCare247 offers a solid out-of-the-box system, though it lacks the D2C subscription checkout optimization found in Bask Health.
