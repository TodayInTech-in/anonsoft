Vendor Guide

How to Evaluate Telemedicine Software Vendors

Use this 4-point checklist to compare telemedicine vendors and secure the best platform for your practice.

📝

1. BAA & HIPAA Readiness

Never rely on verbal compliance. Ensure the vendor signs a formal Business Associate Agreement (BAA). TodayInTech is BAA-ready and deploys on your dedicated AWS/Google Cloud instance.

🔌

2. EHR/EMR Integration Depth

Verify if visit logs, scheduling, and billing codes write back directly into Epic, Cerner, or Athenahealth. We provide bi-directional HL7/FHIR integrations to eliminate manual entry.

💾

3. Codebase & Data Ownership

Ask if you will own the patient data and codebase, or be locked into monthly licensing forever. Unlike typical SaaS vendors, we offer full codebase ownership options for your custom platform.

⚡

4. All-in-One Patient Workflow

Compare if the vendor supports video, intake forms, scheduling, eRx, and payments in one interface. We unify all 5 systems to prevent portal fatigue for patients and staff.
